About DressMe

A student project exploring how photos can help people manage a wardrobe.

Four modules

  • Digital Wardrobe Scan Done

    Photograph one garment, get suggested type, colour and pattern, check and correct it, then save it.

  • Duplicate detection Next

    Spot when a new garment is very similar to one already saved.

  • Outfit recommendation Planned

    Suggest outfits from saved garments using type, colour and pattern.

  • Purchase recommendation Planned

    Advise what may be missing or worth buying based on the wardrobe.

What to expect

  • Predictions are suggestions. You can always correct them.
  • Types covered: t-shirt, shirt, blouse or top, hoodie, long sleeve, polo, trousers, shorts, skirt, outerwear, blazer, dress, shoes and hat.
  • Photos work best with one garment, flat on a plain surface, in good light.
  • Colour and pattern are the least reliable fields. Beige vs grey vs white, and polo vs t-shirt, are common mix-ups.
  • This is a student project, not a finished commercial product.
